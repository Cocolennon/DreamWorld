# Dream World
Minecraft 26.3 mod for Fabric with the goal of adding as much of PillowDear's world into the game as possible

The goal is to get as many piwwos involved in the creation of this mod as possible.
If you are a piwwo and you know how to code, make textures, make sprites or build structures, feel free to DM me on Discord (`coc_`)

# I don't know who PillowDear is
PillowDear is the dream entity who lives in your head to help you sleep in bed!
She's an independent VTuber who plays video games, sings, draws, does handcams and ASMR!
She's also the sweetest girl to ever grace this plane of existence!
